Executive Summary A preliminary geotechnical screening assessment has been prepared for the proposed transmission tower location at Patna, Bihar (25.594100°N, 85.137600°E). The nominal investigation depth considered for this screening is 2.0 m below ground level. Available subsurface information is derived primarily from open GIS soil property models (ISRIC SoilGrids 2.0), aggregated into engineering depth intervals of 0.0–0.5 m, 0.5–1.0 m, 1.0–1.5 m and 1.5–2.0 m. Grain-size fractions, bulk density and related modelled properties are reported with status MODELLED. Laboratory classification parameters (Atterberg limits, SPT N-value, UCS, soaked CBR, field resistivity, groundwater table) are not available from remote sensing alone and are recorded as FIELD TEST REQUIRED or NO DATA. Preliminary engineering calculations (safe bearing capacity and pile vertical/uplift capacities) are presented only where scientifically defensible input parameters exist. Where inputs are insufficient, calculation status is stated as INSUFFICIENT DATA. Settlement assessment requires tower foundation load and is currently recorded as TOWER_LOAD_REQUIRED. Report classification for this location: GIS BASED PRELIMINARY SCREENING. This report must not be interpreted as a field-validated geotechnical investigation unless MEASURED borehole / laboratory data are present and explicitly identified. Overall data readiness: 56.7% of tracked parameters available (51/90). Field validation required: Yes. 2. Scope and Objective of Investigation 2.1 Project Description Project: Transmission line.
